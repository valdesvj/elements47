LOCAL LABELS OF ELEM47
  Written by name in programs_rem (LBL :PANEL:, XEQ :PANEL:); the build gives each a free local number
  00-99 (the calculator files have numeric local labels only). listings/ELEM47_labels.txt lists name,
  number and job.

  Control flow (branch struct): the C47 STRUCT commands (IF / ELSE / ENDIF, DO / WHILE / ENDDO, REPEAT /
  UNTIL; the firmware's app note AN0007), indented in programs_rem; the build checks them and numbers them
  (IF 01 ...). No GTO: the labels are the routines and the data.

  In this order (the firmware finds the step after an XEQ again by counting the steps from the start of
  the program, at every RTN: the key loop first, then the routines of a move and of the detail box, those
  that call nothing last):
  (main part)             :INIT:, then the key loop (DO / WHILE until 0): the key wait (DO / PAUSE 50 /
                          KEY? 33 / WHILE / ENDDO), the direction (8 2 4 6), a move, 5 the detail box or
                          :CLOSE:; then 0: the end: PT deleted, CLREGS, CLSTK
  :CURSOR: :CURSXOR:      the cursor (XOR, unrolled); :CURSXOR: again at the last position (off)
  :PANEL:                 name, Z, mass (GRMOD 1 text, spaces after it)
  :RECORD:                the record of Z (one per data label), its first fields
  :CLOSE: :CLSYM:         the box off: the cells under it again, 5 segments (rows 3-7, columns 3-16);
                          :CLSYM: the symbols from the first Z of a segment
  :DETAIL: :DETREC:       the detail box (:DETREC: from the record already read: a move with the box open)
  :CELLS:                 every cell, by segments (the segment list is in the code)
  :INIT:                  the start: PT, the patterns, the cursor on H, the table
  :TITLE:                 title and the key legend (two ATEXT with CR)
  :SEGMENT:               a segment: box (22 unrolled AGRAPH, the right edge of the last one after the
                          segment), number, symbol; PT by STOSEQ
  :SYMPCE:                the next piece of symbols (before Z 1 and at Z 66)
  :CELLPOS:               the position of a cell (rows 8-9 lower), the rows of its number and symbol
  :FIELD:                 the next field of the record
  :FIELDK:                field k of the text "a/b/c"
  :STEP:                  move the cursor, skip empty cells (REPEAT / UNTIL)
  :HLINE:                 298 columns of a pattern (the bottom line, the header bar), unrolled by 37
  :BOXCLR: :STATES:       the detail box: its area cleared; the state names
  :GROUP: :GRPNAME:       the group name

  Data labels, numeric (XEQ IND):
  00-01   the symbols, 65 elements each (2 characters, then a digit = the column in the cell)
  01-60   the records in ELD1 (Z 1-60), 01-58 in ELD2 (Z - 60), one per label:
          "name/mass/state letter/boiling point/configuration" (:RECORD: puts the label in R43,
          XEQ "ELD1" or "ELD2", which does XEQ IND 43)

Folders:
  programs_rem/  the commented source: edit here (the DATA part is written by the build tool)
  programs/      the programs without comments, numeric labels (tools/build_elem47.py)
  build/         the file for the calculator (convert with rejig)
  listings/      numbered steps with the comments (step numbers = lines of programs/);
                 ELEM47_labels.txt: the named labels and their numbers
  python/        c47sim.py (the C47 simulator of Almanac 47), elements.py (the data),
                 elem47sim.py (runs ELEM47: pictures, step counts)
  tools/         build_elem47.py (also the STRUCT check and numbers); build_free42.py (Free42, not on
                 branch struct); build_dev.py (programs_rem/dev -> build/dev);
                 bench_fw.py (the speed in the C47 firmware: T47 instructions, perf)
  tests/         test_elem47.py, test_opt.py (screens against the optimize build, tests/ref/), test_fw.py (the C47
                 firmware, T47), test_f42.py (Free42, f42run), test_dev.py (the dev programs);
                 calc/LTEST.txt (on the C47: the 196-character text)
  docs/          ELEM47_*.png (from the simulator)

Keys: 8 = 53, 2 = 73, 4 = 62, 6 = 64, 5 = 63, 0 = 82 (KEY? codes, as Almanac 47's menu).
