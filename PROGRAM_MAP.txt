LOCAL LABELS OF ELEM47
  Written by name in programs_rem (LBL :PANEL:, XEQ :PANEL:); the build gives each a free local number
  00-99 (the calculator files have numeric local labels only). listings/ELEM47_labels.txt lists name,
  number and job.

  In this order (the firmware finds the step after an XEQ again by counting the steps from the start of
  the program, at every RTN: the routines of a move and of the detail box first, those that call nothing last):
  :MOVE:                  8 2 4 6: cursor off (:CURSXOR:), :STEP:, cursor on, panel
  :CURSOR: :CURSXOR:      the cursor (XOR, unrolled); :CURSXOR: again at the last position (off)
  :PANEL:                 name, Z, mass (GRMOD 1 text, spaces after it)
  :RECORD: :RECSKIP: :RECGOT:   the record of Z (4 per data label, "|" after each), its first fields
  :INFO: :INFOKEY:        5: the detail box, wait, :CLOSE:
  :CLOSE: :CLSEG:         the box off: the cells under it again, 5 segments (rows 3-7, columns 3-16)
  :DETAIL: :GRPNAM:       the detail box
  :INIT: :TABLE: :KEYS:   the start, the table, then the key wait (PAUSE 50 / KEY? 33)
  :UP: :DOWN: :LEFT: :RIGHT:   the direction, then :MOVE:
  :QUIT:                  0: the end: PT deleted, CLREGS, CLSTK
  :TITLE:                 title and the key legend (two ATEXT with CR)
  :CELLS: :SEGMENT: :SEGPOS: :SEGLOOP: :SYMBOL:   every cell, by segments: box (22 unrolled AGRAPH, the
                          right edge of the last one after the segment), number, symbol; PT by STOSEQ
  :SYMPCE:                the next piece of symbols
  :CELLPOS: :CPROWS:      the position of a cell (rows 8-9 lower), the rows of its number and symbol
  :FIELD:                 the next field of the record
  :FIELDK: :FCUT:         field k of the text "a/b/c"
  :STEP: :STEPLP:         move the cursor, skip empty cells
  :HLINE: :HLLOOP:        298 columns of a pattern (the bottom line, the header bar), unrolled by 14
  :BOXCLR: :KELVIN: :STATES:   the detail box: its area cleared, " K", the state names
  :GROUP: :GRP0: :GRPD: :GRPP: :GRPF: :GRPNAME:   the group name

  Data labels, numeric (XEQ IND):
  00-01   the symbols, 65 elements each (2 characters, then a digit = the column in the cell)
  00-29   the records, 4 elements each: "name/mass/state letter/boiling point/configuration|";
          00-14 in A, 15-29 in B (:RECORD: puts the label in R43, XEQ "A" or "B",
          which does XEQ IND 43)

Folders:
  programs_rem/  the commented source: edit here (the DATA part is written by the build tool)
  programs/      the programs without comments, numeric labels (tools/build_elem47.py)
  build/         the file for the calculator (convert with rejig)
  listings/      numbered steps with the comments (step numbers = lines of programs/);
                 ELEM47_labels.txt: the named labels and their numbers
  python/        c47sim.py (the C47 simulator of Almanac 47), elements.py (the data),
                 elem47sim.py (runs ELEM47: pictures, step counts)
  tools/         build_elem47.py; build_free42.py (Free42); build_dev.py (programs_rem/dev -> build/dev);
                 bench_fw.py (the speed in the C47 firmware: T47 instructions, perf)
  tests/         test_elem47.py, test_opt.py (screens against the optimize build, tests/ref/), test_fw.py (the C47
                 firmware, T47), test_f42.py (Free42, f42run), test_dev.py (the dev programs);
                 calc/LTEST.txt (on the C47: the 196-character text)
  docs/          ELEM47_*.png (from the simulator)
