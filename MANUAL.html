Elements 47 v1.1.0: user's manual

The periodic table of the 118 elements on the C47 / R47: the table on one screen, a cursor you move with the digit keys, and a detail box for each element.

Install

  • You need a C47 or R47 with the firmware 00.109.05.00a0.ALPHA (5 Oct 2026) or later: it has the structured programming commands (IF, DO, REPEAT: app note AN0007), ATEXT, GRFNT and PIXEL.
  • From the release zip, copy C47_R47/ELEM47.p47 to the calculator (USB disk) and load it as any other program. It holds three programs: ELEM47 (the one you run), ELD1 and ELD2 (the element data).
  • Before you run it: at the end ELEM47 clears the registers R00-R99 and the stack. Keep anything you need somewhere else first.

Start

XEQ "ELEM47". After a few seconds the table is on the screen, the cursor on H (hydrogen).

  • Rows 1-7 are the periods, the two rows below them the lanthanides and the actinides.
  • Each cell shows the atomic number (small) and the symbol.
  • The empty part of periods 1-3 shows the title, the selected element (name, atomic number, standard atomic weight) and the keys: the digit keys on the left, what they do on the right.

The cursor jumps over empty cells, and the panel shows the new element. Three times 2 and seven times 6 from H:

At an edge the cursor goes around to the other side: 8 on H goes to the bottom of column 1, Fr; 4 on H goes to He; 2 on Ac goes up to Sc.

The detail box

5 opens the box of the element under the cursor: symbol, standard atomic weight, state at room temperature, boiling point, group with its name, and the electron configuration.

With the box open, 8 4 6 2 move the cursor as before and the box shows the new element at once (here 6: from Fe to Co). 5 closes the box, 0 ends; other keys are ignored.

The longest configuration, Og (oganesson):

"-" in a field: no value (the 34 elements without a standard atomic weight, or no data in PubChem).

End

0 ends ELEM47: the normal calculator screen comes back, with R00-R99 and the stack cleared and the matrix PT (the table's map) deleted.

Where the data come from

Standard atomic weights: IUPAC / CIAAW 2024 (abridged, as on the IUPAC periodic table). State, boiling point, electron configuration: the PubChem periodic table. See NOTICE.

Which calculators

ELEM47 needs three commands of the C47 firmware: the structured programming commands (IF, DO, REPEAT), ATEXT and GRFNT.

  • R47 and DM42n with the C47 firmware 00.109.05.00a0.ALPHA (5 Oct 2026) or later: yes.
  • Original DM42 (not n) with the C47 firmware 00.109.05.00a0.ALPHA or later, packages 1, 2 or 3: it should run, but slower than on the DM42n or R47 (older hardware). Its firmware is cut down to fit its flash: the structured commands allow only 10 IF, 10 DO and 10 REPEAT per program there, and ELEM47 keeps within that. Package 4 has no structured commands: no. The DM42 packages change between builds to save space, so check again after a firmware update. Nobody has tried ELEM47 on an original DM42 yet.
  • Older C47 firmware (00.109.04.00b0 and earlier): no, it has none of the three.
  • DM42 / DM42n with the stock firmware, or Free42: use the release v1.0.0 (Free42_DM42/ELEM47.raw): the same table and keys, without going around the edges.
