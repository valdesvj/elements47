ELEMENTS 47 - QUICKSTART (prototype 1, Hydrogen only)

Needs a C47 / R47 firmware with ATEXT and GRFNT (as Almanac 47 v2.x: 00.109.05.00a0.ALPHA,
5 Oct 2026, or a build from master on or after 30 Sep 2026).

1. Convert build/ELEM47.txt to ELEM47.p47 with rejig and load it on the C47.
2. XEQ "ELEM47".
3. The table shows the H cell (top left). Press + : the detail box of Hydrogen opens in the
   middle of the screen. Any key closes it.
4. On the table, any key other than + ends the program.

Registers used: R20-R23, R30, R39. WSIZE is set to 64.
