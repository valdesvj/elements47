ELEMENTS 47 - QUICKSTART

Needs a C47 / R47 firmware with ATEXT and GRFNT (as Almanac 47 v2.x: 00.109.05.00a0.ALPHA,
5 Oct 2026, or a build from master on or after 30 Sep 2026).

1. Convert build/ELEM47.txt to ELEM47.p47 with rejig and load it on the C47.
2. XEQ "ELEM47". The table is drawn (a few seconds), the cursor on H.
3. Keys:
     8 up   2 down   4 left   6 right   (empty cells are skipped; at the edge the cursor stays)
     5      the detail box of the element under the cursor; any key closes it
     0      the end
   The panel at the top shows the name, the atomic number and the mass of the selected element.

Uses R20-R49 and a matrix "PT" (9 x 18), deleted at the end. WSIZE is set to 64.
