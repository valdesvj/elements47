ELEMENTS 47 v1.2.0 - QUICKSTART

C47 / R47
Needs a C47 / R47 firmware with ATEXT, GRFNT, PIXEL and the STRUCT commands (as Almanac 47 v2.2.0:
00.109.05.00a0.ALPHA, 5 Oct 2026, or later).

1. Load C47_R47/ELEM47.p47 (from the release zip, or convert build/pixel/ELEM47.txt with rejig). Three
   programs: ELEM47 (the one you run), ELD1 and ELD2 (the element data, called by ELEM47).
2. XEQ "ELEM47". The table is drawn (a few seconds), the cursor on H.
3. Keys:
     8 up   2 down   4 left   6 right   (empty cells are skipped; at an edge the cursor goes
                                       around to the other side)
     5      the detail box of the element under the cursor; with the box open 8 4 6 2 move
            (the box shows the new element) and 5 closes it
     F1 F2 F3  (box open) the end, with Z, the mass or the boiling point (K) in X;
            a value shown as "-": the end only
     0      the end
   The panel at the top shows the name, the atomic number and the mass of the selected element.

Uses R20-R56 and a matrix "PT" (9 x 18). At the end PT is deleted and R20-R56 are cleared: keep
nothing you need there. Your stack (4 or 8 levels), LASTx and the other registers are kept.
WSIZE is set to 64.
The manual with the screens: MANUAL.html (MANUAL.md on GitHub).

Which calculators
- R47, DM42n with the C47 firmware 00.109.05.00a0.ALPHA or later: yes. Older firmware
  (00.109.04.00b0 and earlier): no (no STRUCT, ATEXT, GRFNT).
- Original DM42 (not n) with the C47 firmware 00.109.05.00a0.ALPHA or later, packages 1, 2 or 3: should
  run (ELEM47 keeps within its STRUCT limit of 10 IF per program), SLOWER than on the DM42n / R47.
  Package 4 has no STRUCT: no. The DM42 packages change between builds to save flash. Not tried yet
  on a real original DM42.
- DM42 / DM42n stock firmware (Free42): use release v1.0.0 (Free42_DM42/ELEM47.raw in its zip): the
  same table and keys, without going around the edges.
