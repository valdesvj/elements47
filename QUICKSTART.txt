ELEMENTS 47 v1.0.0 - QUICKSTART

C47 / R47
Needs a C47 / R47 firmware with ATEXT and GRFNT (as Almanac 47 v2.2.0: 00.109.05.00a0.ALPHA,
5 Oct 2026, or later).

1. Load C47_R47/ELEM47.p47 (from the release zip, or convert build/ELEM47.txt with rejig). Three
   programs: ELEM47 (the one you run), ELD1 and ELD2 (the element data, called by ELEM47).
2. XEQ "ELEM47". The table is drawn (a few seconds), the cursor on H.
3. Keys:
     8 up   2 down   4 left   6 right   (empty cells are skipped; at the edge the cursor stays)
     5      the detail box of the element under the cursor; any key closes it
     0      the end
   The panel at the top shows the name, the atomic number and the mass of the selected element.

Uses R20-R53 and a matrix "PT" (9 x 18). At the end (0) PT is deleted and THE REGISTERS AND THE
STACK ARE CLEARED (CLREGS, CLSTK): keep nothing you need in R00-R99. WSIZE is set to 64.

Free42 (DM42 / DM42n stock firmware, Free42 3.3)
1. Load Free42_DM42/ELEM47.raw from the release zip (exported by the Free42 3.3 core), or Paste
   build/free42/ELEM47.txt in Free42 (PRGM mode) and export your own .raw. Five programs: ELEM47,
   ELD1, ELD2 (the data), E47T and E47S (the C47 fonts).
2. XEQ "ELEM47": the same table and keys. Uses R20-R75 (SIZE 76 if smaller) and PT; at the end
   CLRG and CLST.
